Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026

UICanary has not launched. This policy covers the presale site. Anything about product screenshots is a design intention for after launch, not a feature you can use today, and not a new warranty.

1. What we collect

  • Contact information you give us — e.g. your email address when you join the waitlist or contact support.
  • Payment information — processed by Creem, our Merchant of Record. We never see or store your card numbers; Creem handles payment processing, tax and invoicing.
  • Product data (in the future) — if UICanary launches, the plan is to store screenshots of the URLs and viewports you configure, to detect regressions. That storage does not exist yet.

2. How we use it

To process your presale deposit, send product and launch updates, provide support, and — after launch — operate the visual regression service. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the service: with Creem for payments and tax compliance, and with infrastructure providers (hosting, email). We disclose data if required by law.

4. Data retention

Presale and waitlist records are kept until you ask us to delete them. UICanary does not store product screenshots or baselines today. After launch, the design is that screenshots and baselines would follow the retention setting of your plan, and that you could export or delete them. That behavior is not available, and this policy does not promise it as a shipped control.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing zyy@xingqingnet.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: zyy@xingqingnet.com.
